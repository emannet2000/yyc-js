1. Before you start

The workspace connects three groups of people who share one delivery:

WhoAccount type in the appWhat you use it for
STORE OWNERStoreAsking for deliveries, reviewing prices, accepting quotes, following your goods.
BROKERBroker (shown as "Broker" in the menu)Pricing, approving drivers, offering jobs, handling problems, invoicing.
DRIVERDriverAccepting jobs, collecting and delivering cargo, uploading proof, viewing payouts.

What you need: a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) and an internet connection. Drivers may also install the YYC Driver phone app (Section 20). Your broker or administrator will give you the workspace web address and, for your first broker account, a one-time setup code.

Tip: add the workspace address to your browser's bookmarks or home screen so you can return to it quickly.

2. Signing in and your account

2.1 Log in

1

Open the workspace address in your browser.

2

Enter the email address and password you registered with.

3

Select Log in.

After logging in you land on your workspace. If you cannot see anything you expect, wait a few seconds: the workspace refreshes automatically.

2.2 Create an account

On the login page, choose the option to create an account (Create your YYC account).

If you are a…EnterWhat happens next
Store ownerBusiness name, contact phone, email, password; your store delivery address and receiving contactYou can request deliveries straight away.
DriverYour full name, phone, email, password; your truck make and model, plate or unit reference, truck type, payload (kg), pallet capacity, service area and whether it has a liftgateA broker must approve you and your truck before you can accept jobs.
BrokerBroker accounts are not created from the public pageAsk your administrator. First-time broker setup uses a one-time code from the administrator.
Your details matter. Brokers use the phone and address you enter to contact you about jobs. Keep them up to date in your profile.

2.3 Forgotten password

  • On the login page, choose Forgot password? and follow the steps. If email reset is switched on, you will receive a link.
  • If you do not receive a link, contact your broker. They will verify who you are and send you a one-time recovery link. The link expires after 30 minutes and works once.
  • When you set a new password, all your other signed-in sessions are ended. Sign in again on each device.

2.4 Log out

Use Log out at the bottom of the menu, especially on a shared computer. Your phone app (if paired) keeps sharing until you stop it (Section 20).

3.2 Action required first

The Action required list (called Work queue for brokers) shows what is waiting for you, with the most urgent at the top. Open it first each time you sign in. Select Review beside any item to go straight to it.

3.3 Searching and filtering

Use the search box to find a delivery by reference (for example DEL-0042), address, cargo or customer. Use the status menu to show only deliveries at one stage. Choose Clear filters to see everything again.

3.4 Changes appear automatically

Screens refresh about every ten seconds and when you return to the page. If someone else changes a delivery you are looking at, the screen updates. If you were editing the same delivery, your typed entries are kept so you can review them and send them again.

4. Understanding delivery statuses

Every delivery has a status. It tells everyone where the job stands and what must happen next.

StatusWhat it meansWho acts next
Awaiting quoteThe request was submitted. No price yet.Broker prices it
Quote readyA price has been offered.Store accepts (or asks for a change)
Ready to assignThe store accepted. A driver is being found.Broker offers the job
OfferedA driver has been sent the job.Driver accepts or declines (offer lasts one hour)
AssignedA driver has accepted responsibility.Driver records arrival and pickup
In transitCargo has been collected and is on the way.Driver completes stops and delivers
Delivery failedThe drop-off could not be completed.Broker decides: redeliver or return
Returning / ReturnedCargo is going back, or has been returned.Driver, then broker
DeliveredCompleted, with proof on file.Broker invoices
CancelledThe request was closed.Nobody
Rule to remember: a status is a commitment. A price offer is not a deal until the store accepts it. A sent job is not yours until you accept it. Cargo is your responsibility from the moment you confirm pickup.

5. Store owner: requesting deliveries

STORE OWNER Describe the work clearly. The more complete the request, the faster you get a price.

5.1 Keep your store details current

Open Profile (Store profile). Keep your business name, delivery address and receiving contact up to date. These fill in future requests automatically.

5.2 Create a request

1

Select Request delivery.

2

Enter the pickup address (where the goods are collected) and the delivery address.

3

Choose the pickup date and a pickup window (for example Morning or Afternoon).

4

Describe the cargo: goods, weight (kg) and the pallet count. Choose the vehicle type: Box truck or Dry van.

5

Under unloading requirements, say what the receiving site needs, for example a liftgate.

6

Enter the pickup contact, receiving contact and receiving hours. Add an appointment reference if you have one.

7

Add any cargo details and additional instructions. Optionally set a delivery deadline (times are in Edmonton time).

8

Select Submit delivery request. Your delivery appears with the status Awaiting quote.

Be accurate. Wrong weights, pallet counts or addresses are the most common reason for a delayed price. If you need to correct a request before it is priced, contact your broker.

5.3 Review the price

When the broker prices your delivery, the status becomes Quote ready. Open it and check:

  • The customer quotation amount.
  • That the addresses, dates, cargo and equipment are exactly what you asked for.
  • The quote valid until time. Quotes last 48 hours; an expired quote is replaced by a new one.

5.4 Accept the quotation

1

Select Accept quotation.

2

The status becomes Ready to assign. The broker can now arrange a driver.

Accepting does not charge you or transfer money. It agrees to this price for this version of the request.

Do not accept a price you disagree with. Instead, submit an owner request (Section 6) so the broker can respond.

6. Store owner: changing a delivery

Plans change. Use these options rather than contacting drivers directly, so that everyone works from the same instructions.

6.1 Before you accept a price

  • You can ask the broker to review the quote through an owner request (quotation review).
  • You can cancel the request while it is Awaiting quote or Quote ready.

6.2 Owner requests

Open the delivery and use Owner requests. Choose the type that fits:

Request typeUse it toWhat usually happens
Quotation reviewAsk about the priceThe broker may change the price. You must accept the new price.
Destination or cargo changeChange the delivery address, weight or pallet countThe broker approves it and sends a new quote, or declines with a reason.
CancellationStop the deliveryBefore pickup, the job closes. After pickup, the cargo is returned to pickup.
  • Write a clear explanation. The broker reads it before deciding.
  • Only one request can be waiting at a time. An urgent cancellation can replace an older waiting request; both remain in the history.
  • Your current instructions stay in force until the broker responds.
  • Withdraw a waiting request at any time, with a short explanation.

6.3 Withdrawing or cancelling after pickup

After cargo has been collected, cancelling does not discard it. The delivery becomes a return: the driver takes the cargo back to the pickup location and records the return receipt. Your broker will tell you when it is complete.

7. Store owner: following your deliveries

7.1 My deliveries

Lists every request you have made. Each card shows the status, the next step and your customer quotation. Select View delivery for details, including the activity history and any evidence.

7.2 Tracking

Open Tracking to see deliveries that are on the move: the last shared location, the next stop and the estimated arrival time (ETA). ETAs are estimates; they do not account for loading time or receiving delays.

Location can be out of date. If the driver has stopped sharing, the screen shows when the last location was received. Do not rely on an old position.

7.3 Recipient tracking link

Your broker or driver can share a link with your receiving team. It opens a simple tracking page that shows the scheduled delivery and progress, without needing a YYC account. Only share it with people who need it.

7.4 Notifications

Open Notifications for updates: quotes ready, offers accepted, pickups confirmed, deliveries completed, and requests answered. Notifications are kept in the app even when email is not set up.

7.5 Delivery records

Open a delivery to see:

  • Delivery evidence: the photo, signature or receipt uploaded for completion.
  • Previous quotations: earlier prices for the same request.
  • Activity history: who did what and when.

8. Broker: your daily routine

BROKER You coordinate the whole process: pricing, dispatch, problems, accounts and money records.

8.1 A suggested order each day

  1. Work queue (Action required): deal with the most urgent items first. Open exceptions and owner requests appear at the top.
  2. Approvals: review new drivers and trucks.
  3. Work board: see how many deliveries are at each stage and spot anything stuck.
  4. Operations: check live deliveries, locations and ETAs.
  5. Notifications and Billing: clear confirmations and invoices.

9. Broker: approving drivers and trucks

Only approved drivers with approved trucks can receive jobs.

1

Open Approvals. Each driver is listed with a badge: Approved or Pending review.

2

Check the driver's contact details and truck details, and complete your own licence, insurance and vehicle checks.

3

Select Approve or Reject.

4

If something changes later, select Revoke approval. The driver is removed from future offers.

The app records your decision; it does not check documents. Keep your own verification records.

10. Broker: pricing and offering jobs

10.1 Price a request

Open a delivery with the status Awaiting quote. Check the cargo, route, schedule and equipment, and clarify anything unclear with the store first.

1

Select Prepare a quotation.

2

Enter the Customer quote (CAD): the price the store will pay.

3

Enter the Driver rate (CAD): what the driver will be paid. The store never sees this figure, and the driver never sees the customer price.

4

Select Issue quotation. The status becomes Quote ready.

Amounts are entered in dollars and cents, for example 485.00. Fractions of a cent are not accepted.

10.2 Correct a quotation

Before pickup, you can change the amounts on a quote that has been issued: select Correct quotation, enter the new amounts and a reason. The delivery returns to Quote ready and the store must accept the corrected version. Any driver offer is cleared at that point. Corrections are not possible after pickup.

10.3 Offer the job to a driver

1

With the status Ready to assign, select Send job offer.

2

Open Review driver availability. Each driver is marked Available, or shows the reasons they cannot take this job: not approved, not enough capacity, no liftgate, or a clash with another pickup window.

3

Choose an approved, suitable and available driver and send the offer. The status becomes Offered.

Offers last one hour. If the driver does not answer, the offer expires and the job returns for another driver. You can withdraw an offer at any time, with a reason.

11. Broker: problems, rescheduling and returns

11.1 Respond to an exception

A driver can report a problem (for example damaged cargo, a closed receiving site or an unsafe unloading area). It appears on the delivery as an open exception and stops progress until you resolve it. Open the delivery, read the reason and any evidence, then enter a Resolution. Be specific: the driver and the audit record depend on it.

11.2 Reschedule a pickup

If the pickup date has passed, the delivery is flagged. Choose Reschedule delivery and enter a new date. The store must accept again, and the driver commitments are cleared, so offer the job again afterwards.

11.3 Failed delivery: redeliver or return

When a driver records a failed attempt, the delivery shows Delivery failed. Choose one option:

  • Arrange redelivery: set a new attempt. The driver keeps the cargo.
  • Arrange cargo return: the cargo goes back to pickup. The driver records the return location arrival and the receiving person's name.

11.4 Hand over to another driver

1

On an assigned or in-transit job, select Arrange driver handover.

2

Choose an approved, suitable replacement driver and enter the reason.

3

The original driver remains responsible until the replacement accepts. Then responsibility moves, and progress and the customer's accepted price are kept.

4

Arrange the physical handover of the cargo yourself. The app records responsibility; it does not move goods.

Use handovers only when a real transfer of responsibility is happening.

11.5 Change a driver's rate

Before a driver is offered the job, you can change the driver rate immediately. Once a driver is offered or assigned, the new rate is sent to that driver as a proposal. They can accept it or keep their existing rate. Changing only the driver rate does not change the customer's accepted price.

12. Broker: invoices, payments and reports

12.1 Issue an invoice

Completed deliveries and returns appear in Action required as ready to invoice. Select Open invoice form, check the job, and issue the invoice. The app confirms with "Invoice issued."

12.2 Record payments

EntryUse it for
Customer paymentMoney received from the store for an invoice
Refund or adjustmentMoney returned, or a correction
Driver payoutMoney paid to a driver from their share
The app does not move money. Record an entry only after you have confirmed the payment in your bank or payment system, and include that system's reference.

A completed delivery does not mean the customer has paid. A customer payment does not mean the driver has been paid. Keep these separate.

12.3 Split a driver's pay

If a job has more than one driver after a handover, use Driver payout allocation to divide the agreed total. Enter a reason for each share. A single driver receives the full rate by default.

12.4 Reports and export

Open Reports. Choose a pickup date range or a status, select Apply report filters, and then Export CSV to download the results for a spreadsheet.

13. Broker: managing accounts

Open Accounts to see stores, drivers and other brokers.

  • Add a broker: select Add broker account, enter full name, email, contact phone and a password (typed twice), then select Create broker account. Broker accounts can do everything you can do.
  • Disable an account: enter a reason and select Disable account. The person is signed out, their unaccepted offers are withdrawn, and accepted jobs stay assigned for review.
  • Enable an account: enter a reason and select Enable account.
  • Help someone regain access: after verifying their identity through a trusted route, enter a reason and select Create password reset link. Send the link privately. It expires after 30 minutes and works once.
Safeguards: you cannot disable your own account, and the last active broker cannot be disabled.

14. Driver: getting approved

DRIVER You can see the app after you register, but you cannot accept jobs until a broker approves you and your truck.

14.1 Your profile

Open Truck profile to check your contact details, truck make and model, plate or unit reference, truck type, payload (kg), pallet capacity, service area and liftgate. Keep these accurate. Changes that affect capacity or equipment need your broker's review before you can take matching jobs.

14.2 Waiting for approval

Your account shows Pending review until approved. If your broker rejects it, contact them to discuss what is needed.

Once approved, your Job offers page will show offers sent to you.

15. Driver: accepting and starting a job

15.1 Respond to an offer

Offers appear under Job offers and in Action required. Each shows the pickup and drop-off addresses, dates and windows, cargo, weight, pallets, handling needs, and your rate. Customer prices are never shown to drivers.

15.2 Start the job

1

Select Accept job. The status becomes Assigned.

2

At the pickup, select Record arrival.

3

When the cargo is loaded and checked, select Confirm pickup. The status becomes In transit, and the cargo is now your responsibility.

Only confirm pickup if the cargo is actually on your truck. If it is missing, damaged or wrongly described, report an exception instead. You cannot confirm pickup while an exception is open, or while you are already carrying another live load.

16. Driver: on the road

16.1 Route and stops

The Route and stops panel shows your starting point, pickup, each stop in order, the distance, driving time (when available) and the estimated arrival at the next stop.

  1. Follow the stops in the order shown.
  2. At each stop, select Complete next stop once it is done. Record the stop's receipt and receiving contact where asked.

Only the broker can change the list of stops once you have accepted the job. Contact them if a stop is wrong.

16.2 Sharing your location

When you start a job, your location can be shared so the store and broker can see progress. In a browser, sharing stops if the phone locks or the browser is closed. For reliable sharing, use the Android app (Section 20).

Estimated arrival times are estimates. Call the receiving contact if you are running late.

17. Driver: delivering and proof

17.1 Before you can confirm delivery

  1. All intermediate stops are completed.
  2. Arrival at the final destination is recorded.
  3. There is no open exception.
  4. A proof file is uploaded after you arrive at the final destination. Accepted: PNG, JPEG or PDF, up to 2 MB each. Use Evidence files to add files. Up to ten general files can be added, with space kept for the completion proof.
  5. You enter the receipt reference and the recipient name.

17.2 Confirm the delivery

When everything is ready, select Confirm delivery. The status becomes Delivered.

Proof must be new. A photo taken or uploaded before pickup does not count. A typed name or a file name alone cannot complete a delivery.

18. Driver: problems, returns and rate offers

18.1 Report a problem

If something stops you (damaged goods, a closed dock, an unavailable contact, unsafe unloading), report an exception from the delivery screen with a short description. Your broker is notified and will resolve it. Do not attempt the delivery until the exception is resolved, unless your broker tells you to.

18.2 Delivery attempt failed

If you are In transit and cannot complete the drop-off, select Delivery attempt failed and explain what happened. Keep the cargo safe. Your broker will arrange a redelivery or a return and tell you which.

18.3 Return the cargo

  1. Go to the return location shown on the delivery. Only unfinished stops are removed from the route.
  2. Select Record arrival at the return location.
  3. Enter the return receipt reference and the name of the person who received the cargo.

The delivery then becomes Returned.

18.4 A new rate is offered to you

If your broker proposes a new rate on a job you already have, the screen shows Review the proposed driver rate. You can:

  • Accept the new rate, or
  • Keep your existing rate.

Either way your assignment stays in place. The existing rate applies until you accept a new one.

18.5 Handed over to you

If your broker hands a job over to you, the screen shows Review driver handover. Accept only if you are ready to take responsibility, including the physical cargo. You can also decline. The original driver stays responsible until you accept.

19. Driver: payouts and history

  • Payouts shows only your own payout allocations and payment records.
  • Delivery history lists your past jobs, with the status and evidence on file.
  • Payment records are entered by your broker after they verify the payment. If something looks wrong, contact them with the delivery reference.

20. Android phone app for drivers

The YYC Driver app on an Android phone keeps sharing your location even when the screen is locked or the browser is closed. It works for one assigned delivery at a time. Everything else (accepting, arriving, confirming) is still done in the workspace.

20.1 Pair your phone

  1. In the workspace, sign in as the approved driver and open your assigned delivery.
  2. Under Background location sharing, select Pair Android phone and note the code.
  3. In YYC Driver, enter the workspace web address, the code and a name for your phone, then tap Pair this phone.
  4. Tap Start background location sharing while the app is open. Allow location (precise location gives better arrival estimates) and notifications.

20.2 Stopping

  • A notification shows while sharing. Tap Stop sharing to pause it.
  • Disconnect this phone removes the link from your phone.
  • Sharing also ends when the delivery is completed or your assignment changes.
If sharing stops, reopen YYC Driver and tap Start. Some phones restrict apps to save battery; allow YYC Driver to run in the background and keep location set to allowed.

21. Working without signal

Drivers can keep working briefly without a connection. Actions are kept on the device and sent when the connection comes back.

  • Offline, you can record arrival, attach files and confirm delivery, in that order.
  • Saved on your phone is not the same as confirmed. Check that the delivery shows as Delivered once you are back online.
  • If the broker has changed the job while you were offline, syncing stops and the job is flagged for review. Contact your broker; do not try to force it.
  • Large photos use phone storage. Keep an eye on available space.
  • Entries you have typed (such as a receipt number or recipient name) are kept only while the page stays open. Finish them before reloading or closing the page.
