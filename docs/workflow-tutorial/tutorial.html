1. The three roles

Every delivery passes through three people. Each one only sees what they need to do their part.

Before anyone can work: the first broker account is created once, by the operator, using a one-time setup token. Drivers register and wait for broker approval before they can accept jobs. Their truck is approved separately.

2. The delivery lifecycle at a glance

A delivery has a status that tells everyone what happens next. The main path is shown below; side paths follow.

Rule of thumb: each status is a commitment. Quote ready = a price is offered. Ready to assign = the customer agreed. Assigned = a driver accepted. In transit = the driver has the cargo.

3. Step 1 — The store owner requests a delivery

Store owner   Starting status: Awaiting quote

1

Open the form. From your workspace, choose a new delivery request.

2

Describe the work. Enter both addresses, the pickup date and window, the cargo, weight and pallets.

3

Add handling details. Pick the vehicle type and unloading equipment, plus pickup and receiving contacts, hours and any appointment reference.

4

Submit. The delivery appears as Awaiting quote. This does not mean a price is agreed or a driver is booked.

Tip: clear addresses, correct weight and complete receiving instructions speed up quoting. Incorrect figures are the most common cause of a delayed quote.

4. Step 2 — The broker prices the job; the store accepts

Broker then Store owner   Awaiting quote → Quote ready → Ready to assign

Broker actions

1

Open the delivery and select Prepare a quotation.

2

Enter the Customer quote (CAD) and the Driver rate (CAD).

3

Select Issue quotation. The delivery moves to Quote ready.

Store owner actions

4

Review the price together with the shipment details, then select Accept quotation.

Acceptance applies only to the current version. It moves the job to Ready to assign and does not charge anything.

Disagree with the price or the details? Do not accept. Submit an Owner request (quotation review, change of destination/cargo, or cancellation) so the broker can respond.

5. Step 3 — The broker offers the job to a driver

Broker   Ready to assign → Offered

1

Select Send job offer. Open Review driver availability to see why each driver is or is not suitable.

2

Choose an approved, suitable and available driver and send the offer. The delivery moves to Offered.

Offers expire after one hour if the driver does not respond. A sent offer is not yet a confirmed job; only the driver's acceptance is.

6. Step 4 — The driver accepts, collects and delivers

Driver   Offered → Assigned → In transit → Delivered

Driver actions — collect the cargo

1

Select Accept job. The status becomes Assigned.

2

When you reach the pickup, select Record arrival.

3

After loading, select Confirm pickup. The status becomes In transit and you now hold responsibility for the load.

Cargo missing or damaged? Do not confirm pickup. Report an exception instead. An open exception blocks pickup and delivery until the broker records a resolution.

Driver actions — deliver and prove it

Proof must be fresh. A photo taken or uploaded before pickup does not count. Upload the proof after you arrive at the final destination.

7. Step 5 — The broker closes the job and invoices it

Broker   Delivered or Returned → invoice issued

1

Find the completed delivery under Action required (or open it) and select Open invoice form to issue the customer invoice.

2

Record payments, refunds or payouts with a reference that matches your external records.

8. Exceptions, changes and handovers (quick reference)

Most deliveries follow the main path. These are the common detours.

Owner requests and driver rate changes

  • Owner requests (store owner): ask for a quotation review, a destination/cargo change, or a cancellation. Only one request can be waiting at a time. The current instructions stay in force until the broker responds.
  • Driver rate changes (broker): before a driver is selected, the rate changes immediately. After an offer or assignment, the new rate is a proposal the driver must accept. Declining keeps the agreed rate.
  • Correcting a quotation (broker, before pickup): requires a reason and changed amounts. The store must accept the corrected quote again before a driver can be offered the job.

Rules to remember

TopicRule
QuotationValid for 48 hours; the store must accept the current version.
Job offerExpires after 1 hour if unanswered.
Driver eligibilityApproved driver and truck, enough weight and pallet capacity, liftgate if required, free pickup window.
Delivery proofPNG, JPEG or PDF, up to 2 MiB, uploaded after arrival at the final stop.
Completion needsAll stops done, no open exception, arrival recorded, fresh proof, receipt reference, recipient name.
MoneyCustomer price and driver rate are separate. Invoices and payments are recorded, not transferred, by the app.
PricingAmounts are entered in whole cents (e.g. $485.00). Fractional cents are rejected.
Need help? If a button is blocked, the app shows the reason in the status panel and the Action required list. Start there before contacting your broker.
